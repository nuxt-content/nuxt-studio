# About

This is a small Nuxt Content application used to test Nuxt Studio with Cloudinary as its external media provider.



## What to try

1. Open [Nuxt Studio](/admin).
2. Go to **Media** and upload an image.
3. Confirm the asset is stored in Cloudinary under the configured folder.
4. Open [Media picker](/media-picker) and select the uploaded asset in Studio.

Uploaded media is stored in Cloudinary and is not committed to Git. Content pages remain managed by Nuxt Content.
